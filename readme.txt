Rule You'll Use Forever

I want you to remember this:

If I'm arranging things in one direction → Flexbox.

If I'm arranging things in rows AND columns → Grid.

That's 90% of the decision.

Padding = inside

Margin = outside

I teach students this sentence:

Padding feeds the box. Margin separates the boxes.

Phase 1: Discovery

Before touching HTML or CSS, understand the business.

Here are the questions I ask every client:

Business
What do you do?
How long have you been in business?
What makes you different?
Who are your competitors?
Customers
Who is your ideal customer?
Homeowners?
Businesses?
Luxury clients?
Goals

Why do you need this website?

Possible answers:

Get phone calls
Generate leads
Book appointments
Sell products
Build trust

Every answer changes how the website is designed.
